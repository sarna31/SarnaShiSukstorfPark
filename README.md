**Project Overview** /n
Access to reliable and affordable electricity is an important component of economic development. This project aims to explore the relationship between electricity access and economic development using the analysis and visualization skills we have developed throughout the first half of the semester.

**Research Question** /n
How is electricity access associated with  economic development across different countries? How do these relationships vary by region, income groups, and other demographic characteristics?

**Data** /n
For the purpose of answering our research questions, we will be using the World Bank datasets. For our final deliverable, we aim to create a dashboard that presents a comparitive analysis of electricity access and development across several countries, including Cambodia, Ethiopia, Honduras, Kenya, Liberia, Myanmar, Nepal, Niger, Nigeria, Rwanda and Zambia.

**Intended Audience** /n
Our primary audience is global development decision-makers and policy-makers, including international organizations and development institutions, such as the World Bank and United Nations, as well as national government agencies. The project is designed to make data-driven insights accessible and useful to audiences in the energy access, infrastructure planning, and economic development spaces. 

**Data Dictionary** 
 - *Country* Country in which the household was surveyed.
 - *HH_ID* Household ID. Used as identification across survey categories.
 - *Subdivision1* Indicates the household's local province.
 - *Subdivision2* Indicates the household's local county.
 - *UrbanOrRural* Indicates whether the household is in an urban/rural environment.
 - *ElectrificationStatus* Indicates whether the household has access to electricity.
 - *HouseholdSize* Indicates the number of people living in the household.
 - *DwellingType* Indicates the type of dwelling the household lives in.
 - *DwellingOwnership* Indicates whether the respondent owns their dwelling.
 - *DwellingRoomsNumber* The number of rooms present in the dwelling.
 - *BankAccount* Indicates whether the household has a bank account with a formal institution.
 - *GridConnected* Indicates whether the household is connected to the electric grid.
 - *NumYearsGridConnected* The number of years a household has been connected to the electric grid.
 - *GridConnectionFeeLocal* The fee for connecting to the grid (local currency). 
 - *GridConnectionFeeUSD* The fee for connecting to the grid (converted to USD).
 - *InternalWiringFeeLocal* The fee for internal wiring (local currency).
 - *InternalWiringFeeUSD* The fee for internal wiring (converted to USD).
 - *ElectricityBillPriceLocal* The price of electricity over the past month (local currency).
 - *ElectricityBillPriceUSD* The price of electricity over the past month (converted to USD).
 - *ElectricityConsumptionkWh* Household electricity consumption in the last month (in kilowatt-hours).
 - *OutageDurationHours* The number of hours of outages/blackouts in the past week.
 - *MobilePhonesOwned* Indicates whether the household owns at least 1 mobile phone.
 - *WillingToPayForConnection* Indicates whether the household is willing to pay for a new electric grid connection.
 - *EstimateOfElectricitySpendingLocal* An estimate of the amount of money spent on electricity in the past month (local currency).
 - *EstimateOfElectricitySpendingUSD* An estimate of the amount of money spent on electricity in the past month (local currency).
