**Project Overview**
Access to reliable and affordable electricity is an important component of economic development. This project aims to explore the relationship between electricity access and economic development using the analysis and visualization skills we have developed throughout the first half of the semester.

**Research Question**
How is electricity access associated with  economic development across different countries? How do these relationships vary by region, income groups, and other demographic characteristics?

**Data**
For the purpose of answering our research questions, we will be using the World Bank datasets. For our final deliverable, we aim to create a dashboard that presents a comparitive analysis of electricity access and development across several countries, including Cambodia, Ethiopia, Honduras, Kenya, Liberia, Myanmar, Nepal, Niger, Nigeria, Rwanda, and Zambia. 

**Intended Audience**
Our primary audience is global development decision-makers and policy-makers, including international organizations and development institutions, such as the World Bank and United Nations, as well as national government agencies. The project is designed to make data-driven insights accessible and useful to audiences in the energy access, infrastructure planning, and economic development spaces.
